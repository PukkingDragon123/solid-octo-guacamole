🦫 DAM IT

You are the valley's beaver contractor. Wild animals keep turning up with a wish-list; your job is to build them a home before they give up and move on.

  1. Hire a crew. Loggers fell, Haulers carry, Engineers build, Gardeners plant and Foragers pick. Every beaver is paid in berries at dawn — miss payday and morale drops until they walk out.
  2. Gather wood. Click any grown tree to mark it for felling. The forest is finite: plant saplings from the Build tab or you will run out for good.
  3. Dam the river. Place dam segments on the channel. When every water tile across the river is sealed, the water rises and floods a pond behind it.
  4. Plant what they ask for. Berries, flowers and reeds all grow in different places — reeds only take in still pond water.
  5. Build the habitat inside the animal's dashed circle, tick off every need, and your new neighbour moves in.

Controls — left-click: mark a tree / place / select a beaver · drag while placing to lay a row of dam · right-click: cancel or demolish · Space pause · 123 speed · Esc cancel

🪵 Log Slam

Slap the log dead centre three times. A good run packs every building site and earns spare timber.
